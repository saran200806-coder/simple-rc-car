- Arduino Uno or Nano (1)
- L298N motor driver module (1)

- Battery pack (2x 18650 holders or 7.4V Li-ion pack) + battery holder
- Jumper wires, soldering supplies, screws

Optional tools
- Multimeter
- Soldering iron
- Hot glue gun
