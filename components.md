# Bill of Materials (updated)

- ESP32 DevKitC (1) — replaces Arduino Uno for Wi‑Fi/Bluetooth built-in
- L298N motor driver module (1) — or TB6612FNG for better efficiency
- 2x DC geared motors with wheels (2)
- Chassis (1) - simple kit or DIY
- Joystick module (2-axis) - optional (1)
- HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor (1)
- Battery pack for motors (e.g., 7.4V Li-ion or 6V-12V pack)
- USB cable for ESP32
- Jumper wires, soldering supplies, screws

Optional
- Motor driver upgrade (TB6612 or VNH) if your motors need more current
- Wheel encoders for closed-loop control
